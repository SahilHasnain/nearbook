## MVP Idea: **NEET Book Marketplace**

### Problem

NEET books are expensive.

* Many students can't afford new books.
* Many previous-year students have books lying unused.

There is no simple, local marketplace focused only on NEET books.

---

## Target Users

* NEET Aspirants (Buyers)
* Ex-NEET Students (Sellers)

---

## Value Proposition

> **Buy and sell used NEET books nearby at affordable prices.**

---

# MVP Features

### 1. Browse Books

* Search
* Filter by:

  * Subject (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  * Book (NCERT, MTG, Allen, Aakash, etc.)
  * Condition
  * Price

---

### 2. Sell a Book

Seller adds:

* Photos
* Book Name
* Edition
* Condition
* Price
* City

Publish.

---

### 3. Book Details

* Images
* Description
* Condition
* Price
* Seller location
* Seller profile

---

### 4. Chat

Simple in-app chat between buyer and seller.

No calling.

---

### 5. Nearby Books

Show listings within the user's city first.

---

### 6. Saved Books

Wishlist for later.

---

# Don't Build in MVP

* ❌ Payments
* ❌ Delivery
* ❌ Ratings
* ❌ AI
* ❌ Book recommendations
* ❌ Auctions
* ❌ Wallet
* ❌ Admin approval workflow (unless necessary)

---

# Revenue

Initially:

* Free

Later:

* Featured listings
* Seller Boost
* Verified Seller badge
* Local bookshop advertisements

---

---

## Success Metric

The MVP has one job:

> **Help a student find and buy a used NEET book nearby within minutes.**

If you achieve that consistently, you've validated the core marketplace before expanding into other exams or adding advanced features.
