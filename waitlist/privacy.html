Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

In brief

Sunni NearBook uses your account details to provide the marketplace, optional approximate location to show nearby books, and an optional push token to deliver activity alerts. We do not sell personal information. Your seller-eligibility answer is stored only on your device.

1. About this policy

Sunni NearBook ("Sunni NearBook", "we", "us", or "our") is a peer-to-peer marketplace for Sunni Muslims to discover, list, and discuss books. This policy covers the Sunni NearBook mobile app and our waitlist page. It explains what information is handled, why, who receives it, and the choices available to you.

2. Information we handle

Account and sign-in information

We handle your email address, account name, and account identifier. You can sign in using an email verification code or Google. If you choose Google sign-in, Google and our authentication provider process information needed to authenticate you, such as your Google account identifier, email address, and basic profile name. We do not receive your Google password.

Listings, saved books, and messages

We store information you submit, such as book titles, subjects, publishers, editions, condition, price and currency, description, photos, saved books, and messages. Your public listings show the information needed by other users to evaluate and contact you. Messages are available to the participants in that conversation.

Approximate location

If you grant location permission and choose to use the location feature, the app obtains device location to determine an area label. Before coordinates are saved to your account or a listing, they are rounded to three decimal places. The area and approximate coordinates help sort nearby listings and estimate distance; they are not intended to identify your exact location. You can deny or revoke location permission in your device settings.

Push notifications

On supported mobile devices, if you allow notifications, we store an Expo push token in your account preferences and use it to deliver alerts about new seller contacts and messages. Notification text may include a person's name, a book title, or message text and may appear on your lock screen depending on your device settings. You can disable notifications in device settings; signing out removes the token from your account preferences.

Seller-eligibility answer stored on your device

The Sell screen asks whether you meet Sunni NearBook's seller eligibility criteria. Your yes/no answer is saved locally on your device using app storage so the app can remember your selection. This answer is not sent to Sunni NearBook's servers or included in your account. You can change the answer in the app; uninstalling the app or clearing its data also removes the locally stored answer.

Waitlist and device permissions

If you join the waitlist, we store your email address to contact you about the launch. The app may request camera or photo-library access when you create a listing and location or notification permission when you use those features. Photos are uploaded only when you choose them for a listing.

Technical information

Our service providers may process technical information, such as network and device data or service logs, to authenticate users, deliver the app, protect their services, and troubleshoot failures. We do not currently use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.

3. How we use information

  • Provide account access, marketplace listings, saved books, and in-app messaging.
  • Show approximate nearby results and estimate distance when you choose to provide location.
  • Create in-app and push notifications about account activity.
  • Send a launch update if you join the waitlist.
  • Maintain, secure, and troubleshoot Sunni NearBook; detect abuse and enforce our rules.
  • Meet legal requirements and protect users, Sunni NearBook, and the public.

Depending on where you live, we rely on your consent for optional permissions and notifications, the need to provide the service you request, our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the marketplace, and legal obligations.

4. How information is shared

  • Other Sunni NearBook users can see your account name and the details and photos you include in a listing. Conversation participants can see messages sent in that conversation.
  • Appwrite provides our authentication, database, file storage, and server functions. Sunni NearBook is currently configured to use Appwrite's Singapore region.
  • Google processes sign-in when you choose Google. Expo's push service and Firebase Cloud Messaging may process push tokens and notification content to deliver notifications to your device.
  • We may disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, Sunni NearBook, or others.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share it with advertisers for targeted advertising.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. They may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

5. Storage, security, and retention

App account data, listings, photos, messages, and waitlist submissions are hosted using Appwrite cloud services. Information is transmitted over HTTPS. We use access controls and reasonable safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Sunni NearBook does not process marketplace payments or collect payment-card details; buyers and sellers arrange payment outside the app.

We keep account and marketplace information while needed to operate your account and the service. If you request account deletion, we will delete or de-identify associated information unless we need to retain some of it for legal, security, dispute-resolution, or fraud-prevention reasons. Waitlist emails are kept until the launch communication is complete or you request deletion. Push tokens are kept until you sign out or your account is deleted. The seller-eligibility answer remains only in local app storage until you change it, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

6. Your choices and privacy rights

  • You can sign out, edit information you provide, and change or remove your seller-eligibility answer in the app.
  • You can deny or revoke location, camera, photo-library, and notification permissions in your device settings. Some features may then be unavailable.
  • You may ask us to access, correct, delete, or provide a copy of your personal information, or raise an objection or restriction where applicable law provides that right.
  • To request account or waitlist deletion, or to exercise a privacy right, email us. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

Your rights depend on your location and may include the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

7. Children's privacy

Sunni NearBook is intended for people aged 13 or older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate. If local law requires a higher minimum age, that higher age applies.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Sunni NearBook changes or legal requirements evolve. We will update the date above and, for material changes, provide an additional notice where appropriate.
